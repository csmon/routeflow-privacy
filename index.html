RouteFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

English 繁體中文

English

1. Overview

RouteFlow is an Android app for simulating a fixed location or movement along a route for development, testing, demonstrations, and personal location simulation. While mock location is active, other apps on the device that use location may receive the simulated location.

RouteFlow does not require a RouteFlow account and does not operate a RouteFlow-owned backend for storing user routes or location history.

2. Information stored on your device

RouteFlow may store the following information locally on the device:

  • Favorite places, including names, optional addresses, coordinates, and timestamps.
  • Favorite routes, including names, route points, coordinates, route settings, and timestamps.
  • App settings such as language, appearance, speed settings, update interval, and map preferences.
  • A small recent-place history for items you explicitly select.
  • Temporary map, route-editing, selection, and simulation state used to restore the current app experience.

RouteFlow does not upload these saved places, routes, or app settings to a RouteFlow-owned server. Android backup may retain or restore app data depending on your device and Google account backup settings.

3. Location and mock-location data

If you grant location permission, RouteFlow can read the device's location for features such as choosing the current location as a route point or showing the device's location on the map.

RouteFlow can also provide simulated location data through Android mock-location mechanisms and Google Play services location APIs. Simulated routes and coordinates are processed on the device and are not uploaded to a RouteFlow-owned server.

4. Advertising and consent

Ad-supported versions of RouteFlow may use Google Mobile Ads to display banner, interstitial, and app-open advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device and network information, advertising identifiers when available, ad interactions, and diagnostics in accordance with their own policies.

RouteFlow uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices where required. RouteFlow relies on UMP's current permission state before requesting ads and does not store consent strings as its own authoritative record.

Where required, RouteFlow provides an Ad privacy choices / 廣告隱私設定 entry in Settings so users can review or change available privacy choices.

Rejecting personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no ads will be shown. Depending on applicable law, consent choices, and Google's serving rules, non-personalized or limited ads may still be available.

5. Maps, search, and network services

RouteFlow uses MapLibre Native for map rendering and currently uses OpenFreeMap-hosted map resources. Loading map tiles, styles, fonts, or other map resources can cause the relevant provider to receive standard network information such as IP address, request metadata, and requests corresponding to the map area being viewed.

Offline country and city search uses bundled GeoNames-derived data and is processed on the device. RouteFlow may also offer an explicit external web search for coordinates. If you choose that action, the search terms are sent to the browser or search provider you select, under that provider's own privacy policy.

6. Third-party services

RouteFlow may use or interact with the following third-party services and libraries:

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent and privacy-choice management.
  • Google Mobile Ads / Google Play services for advertising in ad-supported builds.
  • Google Play services Location for device location and mock-location integration.
  • MapLibre Native for map rendering.
  • OpenFreeMap for hosted map styles and resources.
  • OpenStreetMap and related open map-data sources used by the active map style.
  • GeoNames-derived data for offline country/city search.
  • Your chosen browser or web-search provider when you explicitly launch an external coordinate search.

These third parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Analytics and crash reporting

RouteFlow does not currently use a RouteFlow-operated analytics service or an uploaded crash-reporting service in the public app. If this changes, this policy will be updated before or with the relevant release.

8. Data deletion and retention

You can delete saved favorites from within RouteFlow. Uninstalling RouteFlow normally removes its app-private local database, preferences, and files from the device. Copies managed by Android or a device/account backup service may be retained or restored according to that service's settings and policies.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when RouteFlow changes its storage, permissions, map providers, networking, advertising, billing, analytics, or other data practices. The "Last updated" date above will be revised when material changes are published.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about RouteFlow, you may open an issue in the public policy repository: github.com/csmon/routeflow-privacy/issues.

繁體中文（台灣）

1. 概要

RouteFlow 是 Android 模擬定位應用程式，可用於模擬單點位置或沿路線移動，適合應用程式開發、 測試、展示及個人位置模擬。啟用模擬定位時，裝置上使用位置資訊的其他應用程式可能收到模擬位置。

RouteFlow 不需要 RouteFlow 帳號，也沒有由 RouteFlow 經營、用來保存使用者路線或位置歷史的後端伺服器。

2. 儲存在裝置上的資訊

RouteFlow 可能在裝置本機保存：

  • 收藏地點，包括名稱、選填地址、座標與時間資訊。
  • 收藏路線，包括名稱、路線點、座標、路線設定與時間資訊。
  • 應用程式設定，例如語言、外觀、速度、更新間隔與地圖偏好。
  • 少量由使用者明確選取所形成的最近地點紀錄。
  • 用於恢復目前操作體驗的暫時地圖、路線編輯、選取及模擬狀態。

RouteFlow 不會把上述收藏、路線或 App 設定上傳至 RouteFlow 自有伺服器。 Android 備份功能可能依裝置及 Google 帳號的備份設定保留或還原應用程式資料。

3. 位置與模擬定位資料

若你授予位置權限，RouteFlow 可讀取裝置位置，例如將目前位置設為路線點，或在地圖上顯示裝置位置。

RouteFlow 亦可透過 Android 模擬定位機制及 Google Play services 位置 API 提供模擬位置。 模擬路線與座標在裝置上處理，不會上傳至 RouteFlow 自有伺服器。

4. 廣告與同意管理

含廣告的 RouteFlow 版本可能使用 Google Mobile Ads 顯示橫幅、插頁及 App Open 廣告。 Google 與其廣告合作夥伴可能依其政策處理裝置與網路資訊、可用的廣告識別碼、 廣告互動資訊及診斷資料。

RouteFlow 使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP），在適用地區取得及管理隱私選擇。 RouteFlow 會依 UMP 當下提供的允許狀態決定是否提出廣告請求，不會自行把 consent 字串保存為權威紀錄。

在適用情況下，RouteFlow 的設定頁會提供 「廣告隱私設定」 / “Ad privacy choices”，讓使用者檢視或修改可用的隱私選擇。

拒絕個人化廣告不一定表示完全不會顯示廣告。依適用法規、使用者選擇及 Google 的廣告提供規則， 仍可能提供非個人化或受限廣告。

5. 地圖、搜尋與網路服務

RouteFlow 使用 MapLibre Native 繪製地圖，目前使用 OpenFreeMap 提供的地圖資源。 載入地圖圖磚、樣式、字型或其他資源時，相關供應商可能收到一般網路資訊，例如 IP 位址、 請求中附帶的資訊，以及對應使用者所瀏覽地圖區域的請求。

離線國家／城市搜尋使用內建的 GeoNames 衍生資料，並在裝置上完成。 RouteFlow 也可能提供明確的外部網頁座標搜尋動作；只有在你主動選擇時， 查詢內容才會交給你所使用的瀏覽器或搜尋服務，並依該服務的隱私政策處理。

開發或 Internal 版本可能包含額外的測試服務或診斷功能，這些功能不屬於公開正式版的一部分。

6. 第三方服務

RouteFlow 可能使用或與以下第三方服務／函式庫互動：

  • Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）：同意與隱私選擇管理。
  • Google Mobile Ads / Google Play services：在含廣告版本中提供廣告。
  • Google Play services Location：裝置位置與模擬定位整合。
  • MapLibre Native：地圖繪製。
  • OpenFreeMap：託管地圖樣式與資源。
  • OpenStreetMap 及目前地圖樣式所使用的相關開放地圖資料來源。
  • GeoNames 衍生資料：離線國家／城市搜尋。
  • 當你明確啟動外部座標搜尋時所選擇的瀏覽器或網頁搜尋服務。

上述第三方依其各自條款與隱私政策處理資訊。Google 隱私權政策： https://policies.google.com/privacy。

7. 分析與當機回報

RouteFlow 公開版本目前未使用由 RouteFlow 經營的分析服務，也沒有啟用會上傳資料的當機回報服務。 若未來改變，會在相關版本發布前或同步更新本政策。

8. 資料刪除與保留

你可以在 RouteFlow 內刪除已保存的收藏。解除安裝 RouteFlow 通常會移除裝置上的 App 私有資料庫、 偏好設定及檔案。Android 或裝置／帳號備份服務所管理的副本，可能依該服務的設定與政策繼續保留或還原。

9. 本政策的變更

RouteFlow 若變更資料儲存、權限、地圖供應商、網路、廣告、付費、分析或其他資料處理方式， 本政策可能同步更新。若有重大更新，上方「最後更新日期」也會一併調整。

10. 聯絡方式

如有 RouteFlow 隱私相關問題，可在公開政策 repository 提出 issue： github.com/csmon/routeflow-privacy/issues。
