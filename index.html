RouteFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

English 繁體中文

English

1. Overview

RouteFlow is an Android app for simulating a fixed location or movement along a route for development, testing, demonstrations, and personal location simulation. While mock location is active, other apps on the device that use location may receive the simulated location.

RouteFlow does not require a RouteFlow account and does not operate a RouteFlow-owned backend for storing user routes or location history.

2. Information stored on your device

RouteFlow may store the following information locally on the device:

  • Favorite places, including names, optional addresses, coordinates, and timestamps.
  • Favorite routes, including names, route points, coordinates, route settings, and timestamps.
  • App settings such as language, appearance, speed settings, update interval, and map preferences.
  • A small recent-place history for items you explicitly select.
  • Temporary map, route-editing, selection, and simulation state used to restore the current app experience.
  • Free route-playback eligibility (including first-play status and earned rewarded-ad playback credits), and a locally cached confirmation of Lifetime Pro access. These are stored to maintain or restore app features.

RouteFlow does not upload these saved places, routes, or app settings to a RouteFlow-owned server. Android backup may retain or restore app data depending on your device and Google account backup settings.

3. Location and mock-location data

If you grant location permission, RouteFlow can read the device's location for features such as choosing the current location as a route point or showing the device's location on the map.

RouteFlow can also provide simulated location data through Android mock-location mechanisms and Google Play services location APIs. Simulated routes and coordinates are processed on the device and are not uploaded to a RouteFlow-owned server.

4. Advertising, rewarded playback, purchases, and consent

The free version of RouteFlow uses Google Mobile Ads for banner, interstitial, app-open, and user-initiated rewarded advertisements. Rewarded ads can grant access to an additional route playback; the earned playback credit is saved on your device. Ads and rewards are subject to availability and the applicable consent and eligibility rules. Lifetime Pro removes RouteFlow ads and the free version's rewarded-ad playback requirement.

Google's advertising services and partners may collect or share data such as IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and network information, advertising identifiers when available, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Depending on applicable consent and settings, this information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance under their respective privacy policies.

Lifetime Pro is an optional one-time digital purchase through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment processing; RouteFlow does not collect payment card numbers or operate its own payment server. RouteFlow requests product details and purchase or refund status from Google Play, and receives purchase information, including a purchase token, to confirm and acknowledge entitlements. The app keeps a last-known confirmed Pro-access flag on the device and may recheck ownership with Google Play when the app returns to the foreground. RouteFlow does not upload purchase tokens or transaction records to a RouteFlow-owned backend. Google Play handles transaction data according to Google's own policies.

RouteFlow uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices where required. RouteFlow relies on UMP's current permission state before requesting ads and does not store consent strings as its own authoritative record.

Where required, RouteFlow provides an Ad privacy choices / 廣告隱私設定 entry in Settings so users can review or change available privacy choices.

Rejecting personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no ads will be shown. Depending on applicable law, consent choices, and Google's serving rules, non-personalized or limited ads may still be available.

5. Maps, search, and network services

RouteFlow uses MapLibre Native for map rendering and currently uses OpenFreeMap-hosted map resources. Loading map tiles, styles, fonts, or other map resources can cause the relevant provider to receive standard network information such as IP address, request metadata, and requests corresponding to the map area being viewed.

In the public Google Play build, country and city search uses bundled GeoNames-derived data on the device; in-app Geoapify online place search is disabled. RouteFlow also offers an explicit external web search for coordinates. Only when you choose that action, the search terms are sent to the browser or search provider you select, under that provider's own privacy policy.

6. Third-party services

RouteFlow may use or interact with the following third-party services and libraries:

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent and privacy-choice management.
  • Google Mobile Ads / Google Play services for banner, interstitial, app-open, and rewarded advertising in the free version.
  • Google Play Billing for the optional Lifetime Pro purchase, ownership and refund checks, and purchase acknowledgement.
  • Google Play services Location for device location and mock-location integration.
  • MapLibre Native for map rendering.
  • OpenFreeMap for hosted map styles and resources.
  • OpenStreetMap and related open map-data sources used by the active map style.
  • GeoNames-derived data for offline country/city search.
  • Your chosen browser or web-search provider when you explicitly launch an external coordinate search.

These third parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Analytics and crash reporting

RouteFlow does not operate its own analytics service or enable an app-owned uploaded crash-reporting service in the public app. Third-party advertising services may still process app interactions and diagnostics as described in Section 4. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or with the relevant release.

8. Data deletion and retention

You can delete saved favorites within RouteFlow. RouteFlow does not create user accounts, so it does not offer a RouteFlow account-deletion process. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local database, preferences, playback credits, and private files from that device. Copies managed by Android or a device/account backup service may be retained or restored according to that service's settings and policies. Uninstalling does not erase Google Play's purchase history or information held by advertising and other third-party services; their retention and deletion processes follow their own policies. For privacy inquiries, use the contact email in Section 10.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when RouteFlow changes its storage, permissions, map providers, networking, advertising, billing, analytics, or other data practices. The "Last updated" date above will be revised when material changes are published.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions about RouteFlow, please contact: cstar.app.support@gmail.com.

繁體中文（台灣）

1. 概要

RouteFlow 是 Android 模擬定位應用程式，可用於模擬單點位置或沿路線移動，適合應用程式開發、 測試、展示及個人位置模擬。啟用模擬定位時，裝置上使用位置資訊的其他應用程式可能收到模擬位置。

RouteFlow 不需要 RouteFlow 帳號，也沒有由 RouteFlow 經營、用來保存使用者路線或位置歷史的後端伺服器。

2. 儲存在裝置上的資訊

RouteFlow 可能在裝置本機保存：

  • 收藏地點，包括名稱、選填地址、座標與時間資訊。
  • 收藏路線，包括名稱、路線點、座標、路線設定與時間資訊。
  • 應用程式設定，例如語言、外觀、速度、更新間隔與地圖偏好。
  • 少量由使用者明確選取所形成的最近地點紀錄。
  • 用於恢復目前操作體驗的暫時地圖、路線編輯、選取及模擬狀態。
  • 免費版路線播放資格（包括首次播放使用狀態及觀看獎勵廣告取得的播放權益），以及用於恢復功能的 Lifetime Pro 已確認權限快取。

RouteFlow 不會把上述收藏、路線或 App 設定上傳至 RouteFlow 自有伺服器。 Android 備份功能可能依裝置及 Google 帳號的備份設定保留或還原應用程式資料。

3. 位置與模擬定位資料

若你授予位置權限，RouteFlow 可讀取裝置位置，例如將目前位置設為路線點，或在地圖上顯示裝置位置。

RouteFlow 亦可透過 Android 模擬定位機制及 Google Play services 位置 API 提供模擬位置。 模擬路線與座標在裝置上處理，不會上傳至 RouteFlow 自有伺服器。

4. 廣告、獎勵播放、購買與同意管理

RouteFlow 免費版使用 Google Mobile Ads 顯示橫幅、插頁、App Open 及使用者主動選擇觀看的 Rewarded（獎勵）廣告。觀看獎勵廣告可取得額外一次路線播放權益，取得的播放權益會儲存在裝置本機。 廣告提供與獎勵取得依廣告供應情況、適用同意及資格條件而定。 Lifetime Pro 會移除 RouteFlow 廣告及免費版需要觀看獎勵廣告才能繼續播放的限制。

Google 廣告服務及合作夥伴可能收集或分享 IP 位址（可用於推估大概位置）、 裝置及網路資訊、可用的廣告識別碼、App 與廣告互動資訊，以及診斷資料。 依適用的同意與設定，這些資訊可能用於廣告、數據分析、詐欺防範、安全性與法規遵循， 並依相關供應商的隱私權政策處理。

Lifetime Pro 為透過 Google Play Billing 提供的選用一次性數位購買。 付款由 Google Play 處理；RouteFlow 不收集信用卡號，也沒有自己的付款伺服器。 RouteFlow 會向 Google Play 查詢商品資訊、購買或退款狀態，並接收購買資訊（包含購買憑證 Token） 以確認及認可購買權益。App 會在本機保存最近一次已確認的 Pro 權限狀態， 並可能在返回前景時重新向 Google Play 查詢購買資格。 RouteFlow 不會將購買 Token 或交易紀錄上傳至自有後端；Google Play 依 Google 的政策處理交易資料。

RouteFlow 使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP），在適用地區取得及管理隱私選擇。 RouteFlow 會依 UMP 當下提供的允許狀態決定是否提出廣告請求，不會自行把 consent 字串保存為權威紀錄。

在適用情況下，RouteFlow 的設定頁會提供 「廣告隱私設定」 / “Ad privacy choices”，讓使用者檢視或修改可用的隱私選擇。

拒絕個人化廣告不一定表示完全不會顯示廣告。依適用法規、使用者選擇及 Google 的廣告提供規則， 仍可能提供非個人化或受限廣告。

5. 地圖、搜尋與網路服務

RouteFlow 使用 MapLibre Native 繪製地圖，目前使用 OpenFreeMap 提供的地圖資源。 載入地圖圖磚、樣式、字型或其他資源時，相關供應商可能收到一般網路資訊，例如 IP 位址、 請求中附帶的資訊，以及對應使用者所瀏覽地圖區域的請求。

公開 Google Play 版本的國家／城市搜尋使用內建 GeoNames 衍生資料並在裝置上完成； App 內 Geoapify 線上地點搜尋在 Play 版本中停用。 RouteFlow 也提供明確的外部網頁座標搜尋動作；只有在你主動選擇時， 查詢內容才會交給你所使用的瀏覽器或搜尋服務，並依該服務的隱私政策處理。

6. 第三方服務

RouteFlow 可能使用或與以下第三方服務／函式庫互動：

  • Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）：同意與隱私選擇管理。
  • Google Mobile Ads / Google Play services：在免費版提供橫幅、插頁、App Open 與 Rewarded 獎勵廣告。
  • Google Play Billing：處理選用 Lifetime Pro 購買、購買與退款資格查詢及購買認可。
  • Google Play services Location：裝置位置與模擬定位整合。
  • MapLibre Native：地圖繪製。
  • OpenFreeMap：託管地圖樣式與資源。
  • OpenStreetMap 及目前地圖樣式所使用的相關開放地圖資料來源。
  • GeoNames 衍生資料：離線國家／城市搜尋。
  • 當你明確啟動外部座標搜尋時所選擇的瀏覽器或網頁搜尋服務。

上述第三方依其各自條款與隱私政策處理資訊。Google 隱私權政策： https://policies.google.com/privacy。

7. 分析與當機回報

RouteFlow 公開版本沒有自行經營分析服務，也沒有啟用由 App 自有系統上傳的當機回報服務。 但第三方廣告服務仍可能如第 4 節所述處理 App 互動與診斷資訊。 若資料處理方式改變，會在相關版本發布前或同步更新本政策。

8. 資料刪除與保留

你可以在 RouteFlow 內刪除已保存的收藏。RouteFlow 不提供帳號註冊，因此沒有 RouteFlow 帳號刪除流程。 解除安裝 App 通常會移除該裝置上的本機資料庫、偏好設定、播放權益及 App 私有檔案。 Android 或裝置／帳號備份服務所管理的副本，可能依該服務的設定與政策繼續保留或還原。 解除安裝不會清除 Google Play 保存的購買紀錄，也不會刪除廣告及其他第三方服務所持有的資料； 相關資料的保留與刪除依各供應商政策辦理。如有隱私疑問，請使用第 10 節的聯絡信箱。

9. 本政策的變更

RouteFlow 若變更資料儲存、權限、地圖供應商、網路、廣告、付費、分析或其他資料處理方式， 本政策可能同步更新。若有重大更新，上方「最後更新日期」也會一併調整。

如有 RouteFlow 隱私相關問題，請透過電子郵件聯絡： cstar.app.support@gmail.com。
